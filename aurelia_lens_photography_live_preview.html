Behind The Lens

I’m Aarav Mehta, Visual Storyteller & Fine Art Photographer

With over 9+ years of professional experience behind the viewfinder, my passion lies in freezing unscripted intimacy, natural light, and raw human emotion into timeless works of art.

What started as a childhood fascination with my grandfather’s vintage film camera evolved into Aurelia Lens—a boutique photography studio dedicated to luxury weddings, editorial fashion, soulful portraits, and breathtaking landscapes. Every frame is thoughtfully composed so you can relive the exact feeling decades from today.
